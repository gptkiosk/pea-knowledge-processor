[--:--:--] Harbor Voices: the ice house
[00:00:00] Welcome to Harbor Voices. This week we visit the old ice house on the north quay. It was built in 1884 to keep the herring catch cold on its way to the city. The walls are a metre thick and packed with cork.
[00:01:45] Today the cooperative uses it to store rope, and one very large model of the harbor. Nobody remembers who built the model.
