Good evening. The harbor took in four hundred and twelve vessels last year, of which sixty were under sail.

Dr. Aalto: May I ask how many of those needed a pilot?

About a third. The rest knew the channel, or said they did.

Dr. Aalto: And the ones who only said so?

Two of them met the Mussel Bank. Both floated off on the next tide, a little quieter than they came in.
