Harbor Voices: the ice house

0:00 Welcome to Harbor Voices. This week we visit the old ice house on the north quay.
0:14 - It was built in 1884 to keep the herring catch cold on its way to the city.
0:31: The walls are a metre thick and packed with cork.
1:45 Today the cooperative uses it to store rope, and one very large model of the harbor.
2:10
Nobody remembers who built the model.
