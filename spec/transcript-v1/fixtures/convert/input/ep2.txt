Mara: This is Harbor Voices. Today is a short one about the harbor cat.

Mara: Her name is Bosun and she has lived on the fuel dock for nine years.

Pieter: She was a stray. Now she has a bed in the dock office and a monthly allowance of sardines from the cooperative.

Pieter: The allowance is written into the minutes. Three tins a week, and nobody has ever voted against it.

Mara: Does she go aboard the boats?

Pieter: Only the Kestrel. The skipper says she can tell bad weather a day ahead, because she refuses to leave the wheelhouse.

Mara: And is she right?

Pieter: More often than the forecast, which is not a high bar in this town.
